EnigteeWorld production branding

Put your own branding assets in the Vite public folder:

  public/logo.png
  public/favicon.png

The app uses /logo.png and /favicon.png by default. You can override either
path with VITE_LOGO_URL or VITE_FAVICON_URL in .env, then restart Vite.

Recommended logo: transparent PNG/SVG, horizontal, roughly 160-220px wide.
Recommended favicon: square PNG/ICO, 32x32 or 48x48.
